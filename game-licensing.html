Game Licensing & Credits

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Playovia aims to identify the creators and rights holders of games and media accurately. A game appearing on this website does not, by itself, mean that Playovia owns it or has an exclusive licence.

Playovia originals

Games explicitly identified as “Playovia Original” are presented as original Playovia projects. Their code, artwork and audio may still include third-party components subject to their own licences.

Third-party games

Third-party games remain the property of their respective developers, publishers and rights holders. Where known, source and attribution information should be displayed with the individual game. Users should follow any terms supplied by the game creator or host.

Licence verification

We do not label a third-party game as “licensed” unless permission or a suitable licence has been verified. If a game's rights status is unclear, contact us before assuming that redistribution or embedding is authorized.

Request a correction or removal

Rights holders can submit the game URL, identification of the protected work, evidence of ownership or authorization, and contact details to riderog27@gmail.com or use our copyright report page.
