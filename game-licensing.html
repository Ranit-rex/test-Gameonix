Game Licensing, Credits & Copyright

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This page explains how game ownership, third-party material, credits and rights concerns are handled on Playovia. We want players and creators to be able to distinguish platform features from the games and creative works supplied by others.

Important: A game appearing in the Playovia catalogue does not automatically mean Playovia created it, owns it, has an exclusive licence for it, or is authorized to redistribute every part of it. Rights depend on the specific game, its creator and the applicable permission or licence.

Who owns the games and their assets?

Unless a game is clearly identified as a Playovia original, assume that its title, code, characters, artwork, sound, music, logos and other creative elements belong to their respective developers, publishers or rights holders. A game's appearance on this site does not transfer ownership to Playovia or to players.

Playovia-created material

Material specifically labelled as created by Playovia is intended to identify work produced for the Playovia platform. That label should not be read as a claim over third-party libraries, fonts, stock assets, open-source components or other materials incorporated under separate terms. Each component remains subject to its own applicable licence.

Third-party games and embedded experiences

The catalogue may include games or experiences made by independent developers and other providers. Those creators retain their applicable rights. Where source, creator or licence information is known and verified, Playovia may provide attribution or a link to the relevant source. Some games may load from an external host; the host's terms, privacy practices and technical requirements may also apply.

Do not copy, download, modify, re-upload, sell or otherwise reuse a third-party game or its assets based only on its presence on Playovia. Check the creator's official terms or obtain permission from the rights holder before reuse.

How credits and licences are checked

We aim to publish accurate credits and avoid describing a third-party game as “licensed” unless the relevant permission or licence has actually been checked. If a credit or rights status has not been confirmed, it should not be treated as verified. A link to an external game source is not, by itself, proof that Playovia has permission to host or redistribute that game.

Report copyright or licensing concerns

If you are a developer, publisher or rights holder and believe that a game or asset on Playovia uses your work without authorization, please contact us. Include:

  • The exact Playovia page or game URL where the material appears.
  • A description of the copyrighted work and the material you believe is affected.
  • Evidence or a clear explanation of your ownership or authority to act for the rights holder.
  • Your name and a reliable contact email, plus the action you are requesting.
  • A statement that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the rights holder or authorized to act on their behalf.

Send notices to riderog27@gmail.com or use the copyright and game report page. We may request additional information to assess a report. Where appropriate, we may restrict access to the reported material while reviewing a sufficiently detailed notice.

Request a credit correction or permission review

If you are a game creator and would like attribution updated, a source link corrected, or a permission status reviewed, send the game URL and the correct credit or licence details. Please provide an official source or documentation that allows us to verify the request.

Player responsibilities

Players must respect the terms provided by game creators and external hosts. Do not use Playovia to distribute unauthorized copies, remove creator credits, bypass licence restrictions or claim another person's work as your own. Read the Terms & Conditions for the broader platform rules.
