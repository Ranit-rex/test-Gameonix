How to play Obby Rush

Cross the floating-island obstacle course by timing your jumps, sliding under hazards and reaching each checkpoint. The course has three checkpoints before the finish. Use the solo option for a personal run, or create/join a room to race with other players.

Tips

  • Look ahead before jumping between platforms.
  • Use slide for low obstacles and keep your momentum through clear sections.
  • Touch each checkpoint so a fall does not send you back to the beginning.
