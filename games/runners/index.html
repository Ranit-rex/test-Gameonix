How to play City Rush

Choose a world and sprint forward while avoiding traffic, barriers and other obstacles. Switch lanes, jump over hazards, slide underneath barriers and collect coins as you chase a high score. Explore Neon City, Jungle Temple, Desert Dunes and Snow Valley.

Controls: Use A/D or the left/right arrows to change lanes, Space or the up arrow to jump, and S or the down arrow to slide. On touchscreens, swipe or use the on-screen buttons.
