Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This page explains how Playovia handles information when visitors browse the website and when members sign in.

Information you provide

If you sign in with Google, Firebase Authentication may provide account identifiers such as your name, email address, profile image and user ID. Playovia uses account information to provide member features, protect accounts and maintain game-related services.

Guest browsing and local storage

Visitors can browse public pages without signing in. The website may use browser storage to remember preferences and local gameplay activity. Do not assume guest activity stored only in your browser is a complete or cross-device record.

Service providers and advertising

Playovia may use third-party services for hosting, authentication, databases and advertising. These providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser/device information, cookies or advertising identifiers under their own policies. Third-party ads may use cookies or similar technologies to show and measure ads.

How information is used

  • Operate and secure the website and member features.
  • Remember preferences and game activity.
  • Respond to support, safety and copyright requests.
  • Understand aggregate service usage and improve performance.

Choices and requests

You can browse without creating an account. You may manage browser cookies and storage through your browser settings. To ask about personal information associated with your account, contact riderog27@gmail.com.

Children

Playovia is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. A parent or guardian may contact us with concerns.

Policy changes

We may update this policy as the service changes. The date above indicates the latest revision.
