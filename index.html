A BETTER WAY TO FIND YOUR NEXT GAME

Discover, choose and play on Playovia

Playovia brings a changing collection of browser-playable experiences into one place. Instead of jumping between scattered game links, you can browse the catalogue, search for a title and open its game page to see the available way to play. Game availability, controls and features can differ from one title to another.

Find a game that fits your mood

Start with the game catalogue and use its search and category options to narrow down what you want to try. If you are not sure what to pick, explore the recommendations or use the surprise option to discover a title you might otherwise have missed. Your recently played area can help you return to games you have opened before on the same device or account, where that feature is supported.

Play solo or connect with friends

Some titles are suited to a solo session, while supported multiplayer games let players meet through their own rooms or game-specific joining options. Playovia also includes player connections and party features for members. Sign-in is required for account-based social tools and any game flow that specifically needs a member account; the requirements are shown as you enter those features.

What to expect when you open a game

Choose a title from the catalogue to view its available details and start options. A game may load in an embedded player or open through its supported launch flow. Performance depends on the game, your browser, device and network connection. For the best experience, use an up-to-date browser and allow the game area to finish loading before starting.

Game creators, credits and rights

Playovia is a discovery and play platform, not the owner of every game shown in its catalogue. Third-party games, names, artwork, music and other assets remain with their respective creators or rights holders unless a specific licence says otherwise. Read our Game Licensing & Credits page for attribution and rights information, or use the game report page to flag a rights or content concern.

Play responsibly and get help

Use the Terms & Conditions to understand the rules for using the platform and the Privacy Policy to learn about data handling. If something is not working or you need to contact the team, visit Playovia Support.
