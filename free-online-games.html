How to start playing

  1. Open the Playovia catalogue and browse or search for a game.
  2. Open the game's details to understand what it offers and check its requirements.
  3. Select Play and follow the on-screen controls or instructions.
  4. Use the game's available sharing or multiplayer options when you want to invite friends.

Play in a browser, with no one-size-fits-all setup

Many Playovia experiences are designed to run in a modern web browser. Some games or features may have their own device, browser, connection or sign-in requirements. Check the individual game page if something does not load or a multiplayer feature asks you to sign in.

For a smoother session, use an up-to-date browser, keep your connection stable and follow the controls shown by the game. On a phone, rotating to landscape may help when a game is designed for a wider screen.

Frequently asked questions

Are Playovia games free to play?

Playovia offers a catalogue of games to explore. Access, optional purchases and feature requirements can vary by game, so check the details shown on each game page.

Do I need to download an app?

Playovia is a browser-based gaming website. Many games can be opened from their web pages, although individual games may have additional requirements.

Can I play with friends?

Some games support multiplayer or shared rooms. Availability depends on the specific game, and supported multiplayer features may require an account.

Can I play on a phone?

Many pages are designed to work on mobile browsers, but controls and compatibility differ between games. Check the game instructions and try landscape orientation when appropriate.
